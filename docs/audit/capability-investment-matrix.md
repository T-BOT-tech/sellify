Baseline: `main` at `e216db18ade2a5ffa66aff9dc3096e207251a918`.

This matrix is an investment and boundary assessment, not a declaration that every capability is production-certified. Status is based on current source structure, route/store inventory, merged work named in the audit handoff, and the specific gaps confirmed in this audit.

## Investment conclusion

The best near-term investment is integrity and testability of existing canonical authorities—not more verticals, AI actions, or new platform services.

P0 work includes the confirmed Payment audit/atomicity and Procurement receipt/Inventory atomicity defects, plus the two route authorization gaps (`/sync` write permission and Payment routing-policy read/manage permissions). Preserve Identity and Fulfillment authorization boundaries. P1 work completes Supplier Network evidence trust prerequisites and Discovery temporal semantics. P2/P3 expansion waits until the current core boundaries are verified.

Do not interpret the matrix as permission to extract modules. The route and SQLite dependency maps are the gate for any future modularization.
