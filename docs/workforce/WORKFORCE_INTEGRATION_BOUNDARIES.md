# Workforce Integration Boundaries

## Service Booking

A service booking may reference a workforce member only when:

1. The member is active.
2. The member is assigned to the required resource or location.
3. The member has an available window.
4. The booking passes the existing service-booking lifecycle.
5. A commitment is created through the existing service-resource commitment model.

Workforce must not replace Service Booking.

## Supplier Network

A workforce member may be linked to a supplier participant.

Supplier capacity remains owned by Supplier Network.

Workforce may consume supplier capacity evidence; it must not duplicate it.

## Procurement

Workforce may support assignment of procurement tasks.

Procurement remains the authority for demand, assessment, authorization, and commitment.

## Logistics

Logistics Scheduling remains request-only.

Workforce may provide available personnel or resources, but it must not create logistics tasks or bypass the existing logistics authority.

## Catalog

Catalog/Product remains the authority for product identity.

Workforce must not create products, services, or catalog entries.

## Availability contract

The existing Service Availability contract is configuration-only.

It does not claim a resource is free, evaluate appointment conflicts, reserve time, or confirm a booking.

Workforce must preserve this boundary.
